Our Story

From V1 success to a global, AI-powered health platform

2023

V1 Lessons Learned

VoiceHealth v1 proved the concept — thousands of users screened daily via WeChat. But we identified key needs: multi-language support, deeper clinical integration, and telemedicine capabilities.

2024 Q1

Transformer Architecture

Rebuilt the voice analysis engine on transformer-based models, achieving 15% higher accuracy across all condition categories and enabling cross-linguistic voice biomarker extraction.

2024 Q2

Multi-Language Expansion

Added support for 20+ languages and dialects, making VoiceHealth accessible to users worldwide. Developed language-agnostic voice biomarker features that work across linguistic boundaries.

2024 Q4

Telemedicine Integration

Launched integrated video consultation with specialists, enabling seamless referral from AI screening to professional clinical assessment within a single platform.

2025

Global Platform Launch

VoiceHealth V2 launched as a comprehensive health screening platform with EHR integration, longitudinal tracking, blockchain audit trail, and enterprise API access for healthcare organizations.
